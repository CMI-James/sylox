## What Sylox is

Sylox tracks risk on Stellar/Soroban issued assets and lets people buy
and provide cover against specific failure events on those assets,
such as a depeg, an issuer freeze, or an unbacked mint.

A risk oracle scores each tracked asset from live, keeper-reported
signals: price data, liquidity depth, supply changes, and issuer
actions like clawbacks or authorization revocations. An event registry
turns a sustained bad signal into a proposed event, which can be
challenged before it becomes final.

## Testnet only

Everything documented here runs on Stellar testnet. Contract
addresses, sample assets, and signal data are all testnet values. Data
shown by the app and referenced in these docs is sample data until the
live data service ships - never treat a number here as a real price or
balance.

Sylox is not protection in the legal or regulated sense, and nothing
here should be read as promising a fixed or guaranteed return. Read
"cover" as a plain description of the mechanism, not an insurance
product.
